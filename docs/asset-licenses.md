Toyota Corolla model by [D_U](https://sketchfab.com/DU1701), [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-toyota-corolla-e80-sedan-6254cf268d9b46f79dd2a6511e153891), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The model is scaled and repositioned for the scene.

The supplied camera and Eiffel Tower models have no author or license metadata; confirm their redistribution permissions before deploying them publicly. The project explorer does not reproduce employer interfaces. Record the source and permission for every future addition here before launch.

The Paris case study displays a 9 × 9 grid of raster tiles centered on the Eiffel Tower. Tiles are requested on demand from the standard public tile service with at most two requests in flight at once; tiles are not bundled or prefetched, and the browser follows the tile server's cache headers. OSM building extrusions are intentionally omitted so they cannot overlap the supplied Eiffel Tower model. The map displays a visible link to the OpenStreetMap copyright page, which identifies the contributors and the ODbL terms for the underlying map data.
